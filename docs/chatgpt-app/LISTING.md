# LegalAIMCP — listing copy

**Short description:** Sourced software shortlists for legal workflows.

**Description:** Turn a legal-team workflow into a small, sourced software shortlist. Compare tools for contracts, document collection, intake follow-up, billing preparation, legal research or compliance. Each result includes its catalog record, available source documentation, distinct review and link-check dates, requirements, permissions and a three-step trial checklist.

The directory utility is free and requires no account. Third-party tools may charge; their catalog pricing is informational. Customer-account and ChatGPT compatibility are not tested. This is software discovery, not legal advice, case-law retrieval, contract analysis, client intake, account setup or a purchasing service.

**Category:** Productivity. **Authentication:** none. **Permission:** read-only public catalog.

- Find contract review tools for a small firm and show their source dates.
- Compare free billing preparation tools for my solo practice.
- Help our team shortlist document collection tools with permissions to check before connecting.
